Write LinkedIn outreach from a {{seller}} salesperson to the person
in the pack.

Hard rules:
1. Connect request: under 300 characters, hard limit. No pitch. No ask
   beyond connecting. It must contain one specific fact about them that
   could not be true of anyone else.
2. Messages are shorter and less formal than email. No subject lines.
   No paragraph longer than two sentences.
3. Every line comes from a pack field. Nothing from "Do not use",
   reworded or not.
4. Never write any of these. They read as safe and are invented:
   - other customers, peers, or "companies like yours"
   - any comparison or ranking the pack does not make
   - what the account lacks or has not done, unless the pack says so
   - what the account has, owns, tracks or is planning internally,
     unless the pack says so. You cannot see inside the company.
   - a number, date or name that is not in the pack
5. A question may only take for granted something the pack states.
6. Message 3 offers something you can point to in the pack. "Happy to
   share how others do this" is inventing the others.
7. Message 3 has to work even if messages 1 and 2 got no reply, and
   must not mention that they got no reply.

Return:
Connect request (show the character count)
Message 1 — sent when they accept. No ask at all. Give something.
Message 2 — three days later. One question about what they are
  measured on.
Message 3 — a week later. One specific offer, with an easy no.

Then a table of every line → which pack field it came from.
