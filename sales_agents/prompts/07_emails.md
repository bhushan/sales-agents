Here is the personalization pack, including its "Do not use" list of
rows that failed grading:

{{pack_output}}

Write three cold emails from a {{seller}} salesperson to the person
in the pack.

Open email 1 with the most specific evidence the pack holds:
- if "The person" records what they said or did, open with that,
  quoted or paraphrased accurately
- otherwise open with what changed in their industry
Do not flatter. Pointing at what someone said is research. Praising
them for saying it is filler.

Hard rules:
1. Every sentence either states what a pack field states, or draws a
   consequence from named pack fields. Nothing else goes in.
2. Nothing from the "Do not use" section. Not reworded either.
3. No sentence that would be true of any company in this industry.
4. Never write any of these. They read as safe and are invented:
   - other customers, peers, or "companies like yours"
   - any comparison or ranking the pack does not make
   - what the account lacks or has not done, unless the pack says so
   - what the account has, owns, tracks or is planning internally,
     unless the pack says so. You cannot see inside the company.
   - a subject line stating a fact the pack does not hold
   - a number, date or name that is not in the pack
5. A question may only take for granted something the pack states.
   "Who owns X today?" is inventing X unless the pack has X.
6. Fields marked "(inferred, not graded)" are usable, but a sentence
   built on one is a read of the situation, not a fact. Write it as
   one.
7. Under 120 words each. Subject lines under 8 words.
8. Banned phrases: "I hope this finds you well", "quick question",
   "circling back", "just following up".

Email 1: The opener above. Say what it changes for someone in their
  job. Ask one question. Do not describe {{seller}}'s features.
Email 2: A new angle, not a reminder. Use what changed at their
  company, and what this person is measured on. Mention email 1 in
  one clause at most.
Email 3: Short. Make it easy to say no. Leave behind one useful thing
  even if they never reply.

After each email, a table: sentence → the pack field it came from.
For a consequence sentence, name every field it rests on.
